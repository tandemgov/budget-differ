Drop last year's and this year's committee report, explanatory statement, or bill, as published on GovInfo or a committee website. The comparison runs in this browser tab: the files are not uploaded anywhere.

What works, and how this runs

Only PDFs typeset by the Government Publishing Office are supported: committee reports, Senate explanatory statements, and bills (introduced, reported, or committee prints). Compare a report with a report, or a bill with a bill. Scans and other PDFs are refused.

The page runs the same budget-differ code as the comparisons on this site, compiled for the browser with Pyodide. A large report such as Defense takes about a minute.
