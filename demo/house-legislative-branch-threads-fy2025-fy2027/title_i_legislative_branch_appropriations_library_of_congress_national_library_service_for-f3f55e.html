figures updated FY2027The Committee recommends $67,741,000 for salaries and expenses of the National Library Service for the Blind and Print Disabled (NLS).
unchanged since FY2025The NLS is a free braille and talking book library service for people with temporary or permanent low vision, blindness, or a disability that prevents them from reading or holding the printed page. Through a national network of regional and subregional libraries, NLS circulates books and magazines in braille and audio formats, which are delivered by postage-free mail or are instantly downloadable.
