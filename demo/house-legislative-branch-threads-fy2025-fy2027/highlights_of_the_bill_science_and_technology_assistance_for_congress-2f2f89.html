The Committee notes the interest among some Members in reinstituting the Office of Technology Assessment (OTA), which was de-funded in 1995. The Committee is pleased with both the Congressional Research Service (CRS) and the Government Accountability Office's (GAO) efforts to provide research and policy analysis on current and emerging legislative issues related to science and technology (S&T) and Federal uses and oversight of S&T. The Committee encourages the Director of CRS and the Comptroller General to continue their efforts to meet congressional needs and directs a report within 180 days of enactment of this Act regarding efforts made in relation to bridging the gaps identified in the 2019 NAPA report.
