TITLE III DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY › ENERGY PROGRAMS
The recommendation includes $19,000,000 for the Center for Used Fuel Research.
The Department recently announced the development of Nuclear Lifecycle Innovation Campuses to support activities across the full nuclear fuel lifecycle, including fuel fabrication, enrichment, reprocessing used nuclear fuel, and disposition of waste, using a collaborative siting approach. This action marks an initial step towards potentially establishing voluntary Federal-State partnerships designed to advance regional economic growth, enhance national energy security, and build a coherent, end-to-end nuclear energy strategy for the United States. The Committee supports the Department's efforts to modernize the U.S. nuclear fuel cycle and strengthen America's leadership in advanced nuclear energy. The Committee expects the Department to prioritize acceptance of used nuclear fuel from shutdown reactors in accordance with current contracts.
