NATIONAL NUCLEAR SECURITY ADMINISTRATION substantive

TITLE III DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY
The Department of Energy is responsible for enhancing U.S. national security through the military application of nuclear technology and reducing the global danger from the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. The NNSA, a semiautonomous agency within the Department, carries out these responsibilities. Established in March 2000, pursuant to title 32 of the National Defense Authorization Act for fiscal year Fiscal Year 2000, the NNSA is responsible for the management designing, delivering, and operation of the nation's maintaining a safe, secure, and reliable nuclear stockpile. In addition to ensuring U.S. nuclear weapons complex, capabilities are able to meet Department of Defense (DoD) missions, NNSA is also responsible for preventing nuclear nonproliferation activities, weapons proliferation, and naval reactors. providing militarily effective nuclear propulsion plants to the U.S. Navy.
