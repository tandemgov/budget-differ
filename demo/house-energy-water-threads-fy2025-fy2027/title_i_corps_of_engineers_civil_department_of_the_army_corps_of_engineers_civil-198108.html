TITLE I CORPS OF ENGINEERS CIVIL › DEPARTMENT OF THE ARMY › CORPS OF ENGINEERS CIVIL
The Energy and Water Development and Related Agencies Appropriations Act funds the civil works missions of the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Corps). This program is responsible for activities in support of coastal and inland navigation, flood and coastal storm damage reduction, environmental protection and restoration, hydropower, recreation, water supply, and disaster preparedness and response. The Corps also performs regulatory oversight of navigable waters. Approximately 26,000 civilians and 244 military personnel located in eight Division offices and 38 District offices work to carry out the civil works program.
