Language as of FY2027

tweaked FY2026; moved from REVENUES (FY2026)The Committee recommends $520,000,000 for the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC). Revenues for FERC are established at a rate equal to the budget authority, resulting in a net appropriation of $0.
added FY2027The Committee directs FERC, to the extent possible under applicable law, to partner with the Department of Energy's PermitAI initiative to accelerate the agency's permitting processes.
added FY2027The Committee encourages FERC to work with mandatory conditioning agencies to help expedite their authorities in the licensing process, seeking to limit inclusion of conditions that are not central to power generation or are unrelated to project effects, particularly in areas of grid instability which often see higher kilowatt rates.
added FY2026The Committee directs the Department to work with FERC to develop options for incentive-based (including performancebased) rate treatments for interstate electricity transmission to ensure reliability and resilience, reduce the cost of electricity, and align to state energy mix goals associated with electricity by reducing transmission congestion.
added FY2027FERC is directed to brief the Committee not later than 90 days after enactment of this Act on an inventory and status of FERC's final decision of complaints filed by outside parties within the last 10 years.

1 unchanged paragraph
The Committee recommends $520,000,000 for the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC). Revenues for FERC are established at a rate equal to the budget authority, resulting in a net appropriation of $0.
contains: mandatory direction
The Committee directs FERC to identify, validate, and implement a national real-time grid monitoring service to monitor grid malfunctions resulting in poor power quality, safety and reliability.
contains: reporting requirement
The Committee encourages FERC to include, either in its next State of the Markets report or as part of a new annual publication, a comprehensive review of capacity markets.
contains: mandatory direction
The Committee directs FERC, to the extent possible under applicable law, to partner with the Department of Energy's PermitAI initiative to accelerate the agency's permitting processes.
The Committee encourages FERC to work with mandatory conditioning agencies to help expedite their authorities in the licensing process, seeking to limit inclusion of conditions that are not central to power generation or are unrelated to project effects, particularly in areas of grid instability which often see higher kilowatt rates.
1 unchanged paragraph
The Committee directs the Department to work with FERC to develop options for incentive-based (including performancebased) rate treatments for interstate electricity transmission to ensure reliability and resilience, reduce the cost of electricity, and align to state energy mix goals associated with electricity by reducing transmission congestion.
contains: mandatory direction, reporting requirement, deadline
The Committee directs FERC to provide not later than one year after the date of enactment of this Act a report assessing lessons learned from ERCOT's connect-and-manage paradigm and other flexible interconnect queue rules and to include a section assessing the benefits and drawbacks of flexible interconnect queue rules such as the Energy Resource Interconnection Service.
contains: mandatory direction, reporting requirement
FERC is directed to provide a briefing to the Committee evaluating current FERC actions that contribute to lower transmission costs and any additional authorities or tools FERC believes it requires to address transmission costs effectively.
contains: mandatory direction, reporting requirement, deadline
FERC is directed to brief the Committee not later than 90 days after enactment of this Act on an inventory and status of FERC's final decision of complaints filed by outside parties within the last 10 years.
