figures updated FY2027The Committee recommends an appropriation of $4,283,000 for the Joint Economic Committee [JEC]. The recommendation is equal to the fiscal year 2026 appropriation. The Joint Economic Committee was created by the Employment Act of 1946 (Public Law 79-304). The primary tasks of the JEC are to review economic conditions and to recommend improvements in economic policy. The JEC performs research and economic analysis, and monitors and analyzes current economic, financial, and employment conditions.
