revised FY2027The Committee recommends an appropriation of $225,826,000 for inquiries and investigations by Senate standing, special, and select committees, of which $22,583,000 shall be available until September 30, 2029. The recommendation is $3,410,000 above the fiscal year 2026 appropriation. tweaked FY2027Committee Intern Pay.--Senate committees have the authority and sufficient resources needed to pay committee interns from within funding made available to committees. The Committee reiterates that the significant increase provided to this account over the past several fiscal years will more than allow for committees to pay their interns if they so choose. The Committee advises any committee that wishes to seek additional funding for intern pay to include such an increase in its budget request to the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate, so that this Committee can fully consider the needs of Senate committees under such budgetary advisement.
