revised FY2027Calder Mountain and Clouds Sculpture.--The Committee understands that the restoration and reinstallation of the Calder Mountain and Clouds Sculpture is scheduled for calendar year 2026. The Committee directs the AOC to continue working with outside partners to ensure that this timeline remains on schedule. Further, the AOC is expected to continue providing the Senate Committee on Appropriations and the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate with quarterly updates on the status of these efforts.
added FY2027Temperature Controls.--The Committee is aware that a project to upgrade all variable air volume boxes and replace the associated thermostats in the Hart building has limited the ability of Senate offices to control internal temperatures. The Committee directs the AOC to take necessary actions to allow those offices the ability to exercise greater control of temperature within their own office.
added FY2027Hart Area Renewal Project.--The Committee directs the AOC to provide quarterly updates on the Hart Area Renewal Project [HARP] to the Chair and Ranking Member of the Legislative Branch Subcommittee. The AOC is also directed to engage with all impacted offices and committees to collaboratively develop impact mitigation plans to ensure that the work of the Senate can continue during the project implementation. The AOC is further directed to continue working with the Committee on Rules and Administration to identify and make available space that is not currently used as office space and use such space to temporarily house offices displaced during each phase of HARP.
