Dome Tour Transparency.--The Committee understands the special privilege afforded only to Senators and Members of the House of Representatives to schedule tours of the U.S. Capitol Dome. Due to the uniqueness of this opportunity and the volume of requests, it has become increasingly challenging for Senate offices to reserve a Dome tour within Capitol Visitor Center [CVC] operational hours. Accordingly, the Committee directs the CVC, in coordination with other relevant entities, to provide a report on policies and procedures associated with Dome tours and to develop recommendations for how the reservation process can become more efficient, to include recommendations for an online booking platform and opportunities for Senator specific tours outside of normally established operational hours. The report should be provided no later than 60 days after enactment of this Act and should include short and long-term recommendations for actions that can be implemented to improve equitable access to Senate offices to reserve Dome tours. Further, the report should include recommendations for providing the Senate Sergeant-at-Arms with the ability to conduct Dome tour during hours outside of the operations of the CVC.
