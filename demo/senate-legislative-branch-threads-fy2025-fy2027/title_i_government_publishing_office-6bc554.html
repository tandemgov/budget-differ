GOVERNMENT PUBLISHING OFFICE

TITLE I
Legislative-Branch (senate), FY2025–FY2027
FY2026 substantive
 FY2027 substantive

Language as of FY2027

unchanged since FY2025The U.S. Government Publishing Office [GPO] is the Federal Government's primary centralized resource for gathering, cataloging, producing, providing, and preserving published information in all its forms. By law and tradition, the GPO's mission is to provide expert publishing and printing services to all three branches of Government; to provide, in partnership with Federal depository libraries, permanent public access to the printed and electronic information products of all three branches of the Federal Government; and to sell copies of authentic printed and electronic documents and other government information products to the public. GPO's core mission dates to 1813 when Congress determined that information regarding the work of the Federal Government should be available to all Americans. Over 200 years later, GPO's mission remains unchanged. However, the means for producing and distributing that information has transformed dramatically with the advent of the digital revolution. Today, GPO provides free online public access to more than 1.5 million searchable titles, including the Budget of the U.S. Government, the Code of Federal Regulations, the Congressional Record, and congressional bills, hearings, and reports. GPO also provides access to Government information in digital format through apps, eBooks, and related technologies. Since 2009, GPO has experienced more than 1.5 billion digital retrievals from its Federal Digital System, and in February 2019 GPO introduced the next generation of its digital information system, govinfo.gov, symbolizing the evolution of how Americans access Government information in the digital era.
revised FY2027GPO Printing Modernization.--The Committee continues its interest in the pace of modernization at GPO, to include the adoption of commercial, off-the-shelf technology which may present advantages in operational and fiscal efficiency. Within 90 days of enactment of this act, the Committee directs GPO to submit to this Committee a detailed report on the agency's current print practices; how GPO continues to enable agencies to use color, cameras, modern interfaces and software; and other existing solutions and technology to improve agencies' communications with taxpayers, beneficiaries, veterans and citizens.
added FY2027Hosting and Preservation of Inspector General Reports.--The Committee recognizes that legislative branch inspector generals may experience challenges in hosting their reports on websites independent from their parent agency. The GPO is directed to provide the Committee with a report on the capability of the GPO to digitally host and preserve legislative branch inspector general reports on its website. This report should include the necessary resources needed to provide this service, as well as any anticipated challenges.
figures updated FY2026Office of Inspector General.--The GPO Office of Inspector General is provided funding to support no less than 25 FTE and $7,000,000 in operating funds for fiscal year 2026 from within the GPO's annual appropriation. The GPO shall prioritize the resolution of open recommendations from the GPO Office of Inspector General and shall identify for the Committee on Appropriations the reasons why the full implementation of the recommendation cannot or should not be achieved.
